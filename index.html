Pelinsu Kaleli

Computer engineer · Ankara, Türkiye

I work with Unity, Unreal Engine, and Python. I'm interested in computer graphics and procedural generation, particularly tools for building environments and levels.

For my graduation project, RIDGE, I worked on vegetation detection and the Unity pipeline for generating terrain from satellite data. I'm now developing PlayableSketch, which turns hand-drawn floor plans into editable layouts for Unreal Engine.

I graduated from Başkent University in 2026 with a BEng in Computer Engineering (GPA 3.83/4.00, first in my program). Before that, I studied industrial design at TED University. I'm looking for a research-based MSc starting in Fall 2027.
